# Maverick Ventures design notes

The approved layout, Rubik fonts, hero background, overlaps, triangles, reveal animations, capability tabs and responsive breakpoint are preserved. PDF gold/navy replace red. Dark text on gold maintains readability.

Eight service names use two columns within a bounded rounded dropdown, with imagery on the right. The existing transparent pointer bridge and delayed close are retained. Mobile uses the existing accordion structure.

New components are limited to requested service cards/lists, gold/grey/gold MEP cards, certificate gallery, lightbox, contact map and service dropdown. The four category icons are locally authored vectors representing the PDF's Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Buildings and Swimming Pools categories.
